        📜 Worker Rights Guide / የሰራተኛ መብቶች
      


      

        
⏰ Working Hours (Art. 61)

        

          Normal working hours shall not exceed 8 hours per day or 48 hours per week.
        

      


      

        
💵 Overtime Rates (Art. 68–69)

        

          1.5x for standard workday overtime. 2.0x for rest-days and declared public holidays.
        

      


      

        
🤰 Maternity Leave (Art. 67)

        

          90 consecutive days paid maternity leave, with minimum 45 days after delivery.
        

      


      

        
📅 Annual Leave (Art. 65)

        

          Every employee is entitled to 1 working day leave for each month of service.
        

      


      

        
🛡️ Protection Against Forced Labour (Art. 42)

        

          Forced or compulsory labour is strictly prohibited by law.
